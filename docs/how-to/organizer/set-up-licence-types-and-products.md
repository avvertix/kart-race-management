# Set up licence types, products and prices

## Licence Types

Each Licence Provider has its own list of Licence Types, e.g. the ACI driver, competitor and mechanic licences and their sub-types.

1. Pick the provider (ACI or ASI).
2. Add a Licence Type with its name and role (driver, competitor or mechanic).
3. For ACI, list the common **Licence Grades** for each level, e.g. *C Junior* and *C Senior* for national, *International B* for international. Applicants pick one or write another. The level decides the race registration licence category (*national* or *international*), which keeps race registration and exports working as they do today. ASI Licence Types have no level; list their grades and choose the race registration licence category they count as.
4. List the documents a request needs. Add the extra documents needed when the holder is underage. For example, for an ACI driver licence:
   - **adult**: identity card and fiscal code, a karting competitive medical certificate, delegation form, privacy form;
   - **underage**: identity card and fiscal code of the minor and of both parents, a karting competitive medical certificate, parental authorisation, delegation form, privacy form signed by both parents.
5. Attach the forms to be signed (delegation, privacy, parental authorisation). They are given to the applicant pre-filled with their details.

## Products and Prices

A **Product** is anything sold: a licence emission or renewal, a championship enrollment, or a **Promotional Package**. Active Products are listed publicly, with their current Price. Each Product has its own public page showing what is included, the price and, for licences, the documents needed. Share its link in your announcements. Ordering requires a signed-in User with a verified email, and the page explains why.

1. Create the Product and choose its kind. Link it to a Licence Type (licence products) or a championship (enrollment products).
2. Add a **Price** with the dates it applies to and either:
   - a **fixed amount**, charged as is; or
   - a **range** (from–to), when the final amount depends on the Licence Grade. You confirm the final amount for each request after reviewing it.
   For an early-bird price, add a second Price with earlier dates.

## Promotional Packages

A Package is a Product that grants several things once its Order is paid. For example:

- a driver licence renewal;
- an enrollment in a championship, with the right to reserve a race number;
- an Entitlement of 2 race fees in that championship.

Add each grant to the Package. Everything is granted when you confirm the payment; what needs a Licence (the Entitlement, the enrollment) is granted once the Licence is emitted.

When a Package includes a licence and an enrollment, the applicant picks the race number already in the Licence Request. Submitting the request creates the Enrollment, waiting for the licence, with the number held for them. If you reject the request, the Enrollment is rejected too and the number is released.
