# Confirm enrollments and race numbers

Race numbers are first confirmed, first served. Confirm enrollments in the order they were submitted.

1. **Open the championship's enrollments** and filter by *submitted*. Each row shows the licence and whether it is verified, the Competitor, the race number, the payment and *To confirm*: either *Ready to confirm* or what is still missing.
2. **Open an enrollment and check:**
   - the Licence is a driver licence valid for the championship year. If the enrollment still waits for a Licence Request, wait;
   - a Declared Licence is verified. Verify it from the same screen;
   - the Competitor is named, if the Driver is underage. You can name or change it from the same screen;
   - the payment is complete, if the option chosen is paid: a deposit alone is not enough. To change the option, e.g. from Silver to Gold or when the licence category changed on renewal, choose *Change option* until the Enrollment is paid in full, also after enrollment closes: what was paid counts towards the new amount, and the licence renewal cover and the race number follow what the new option includes. The payment proof shows on the same screen: *Confirm payment* or *Reject payment*, so the Driver uploads a new one.
3. **Confirm the enrollment.** A race number the Driver picked and still pending is confirmed with it. You can also confirm the number on its own beforehand, e.g. before the payment arrives; it is released if the enrollment is withdrawn or rejected. The Driver's registrations for races without results are re-evaluated for points eligibility.
4. **Otherwise, reject it** with a reason, which releases the race number and returns a licence request the option covered to its own payment. While a payment proof waits for review, confirm or reject the payment first. The enrollment page lists what is still missing, and offers *Confirm enrollment* only when nothing is.

## Keep last season's numbers

- **Any championship**: before opening enrollment, copy the reservations from last season's championship. They are matched by licence number, for every provider, and confirmed straight away. A driver whose number changed, e.g. with a new ASI licence, is found under *Previous numbers* by holder instead.
- **Any championship**: on each Enrollment, *Previous numbers* lists what the Driver used in past championships, with how each was matched. Choose *Confirm number* for the picked one, or *Use number* for one that is still free.

## Enroll a driver yourself

You can create an Enrollment for any Licence until the enrollment period closes, also before it opens, e.g. for a driver who asked by email. Choose *Enroll a licence* from the championship's enrollments. Nothing is due: settle any payment with the driver. After the period closes nobody enrolls a new Licence.

## Withdrawn enrollments

A confirmed Enrollment is final and can't be withdrawn. A submitted one can, by the driver or by you. Until the championship's first race starts, recording a payment on a withdrawn Enrollment brings it back, e.g. when the driver pays the balance late. If the driver won't pay the balance, choose *Turn into race credit*: what was paid becomes a race fee balance on the driver's licence, less the race fees the option's discounts already covered, and the unused discounts are cancelled. The Enrollment can't come back afterwards.

## Release or change a race number

A pending number stays held until you confirm or release it. To give the number to someone else, release it first. Once you confirm a number, on its own or with its enrollment, only an organizer can change it; the Driver no longer can. From the enrollment page you can still change it while the enrollment is submitted, and it stays confirmed.

1. **Open the championship's Race Number Reservations** and open the reservation.
2. **Choose *Confirm reservation*** for a pending one, ***Edit reservation*** to change the number, or ***Release the number*** to free it.

A released reservation stays in the list as *Released*. It can't be edited, and other drivers can use or reserve its number. While licences and enrollments are in early access, only users with early access see the Confirm and Release actions.

## Recalculate wildcards for a past race

Confirming an enrollment doesn't change races that already have results. To update one, open the race and run *recalculate wildcards*, then reassign points if needed.
