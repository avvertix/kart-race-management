# Enrollment, points and wildcards

An **Enrollment** puts a Driver's Licence into one championship. It is always optional for racing: anyone with a valid licence can register for a single race. What enrollment gives a Driver depends on the championship's settings:

- **Kart championships** usually use enrollment to reserve a **Championship BIB** and to receive promotional discounts. Enrollment may be free.
- **Moto championships** usually use enrollment to score championship points, and charge a fee for it.

## Enrollment and points

Whether a Participant scores points is decided by the championship's wildcard configuration. With the "based on enrollment" wildcard rule, a Participant whose Licence has a **confirmed** Enrollment scores points. Everyone else races as a **Wildcard**. When the wildcard configuration is off, nobody is called a wildcard: they are all simply participants.

Points are scored per category. A Participant can change category between races, and points don't transfer between categories.

## Enrolling mid-season

A Driver can race unenrolled and enroll later. When the organizer confirms (or revokes) an Enrollment, the Driver's registrations for races without results yet are re-evaluated. Races already run keep their status, unless the organizer chooses to recalculate wildcards for that race from the race page.

## Enrollment and the Competitor

An Enrollment can name a Competitor, and must name one when the Driver is underage. The Competitor is usually the parent's competitor licence, managed by the same User. It is not fixed: each race registration can name a different Competitor, and the form pre-fills the one used in the previous race.

## When the licence is not valid yet

A Driver can enroll before their licence for the championship year exists, e.g. while a renewal is being processed. The Enrollment then waits for the licence. Any race number they picked stays held for them, but it is not confirmed until the licence is valid and the organizer confirms the Enrollment.
