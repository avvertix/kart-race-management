# Enroll in a championship and reserve a race number

Enrollment is optional: you can always register for single races. Depending on the championship, enrolling lets you reserve your race number, receive promotional discounts, or score championship points.

1. **Sign in** and open the championship.
2. **Choose the licence** to enroll, from the ones in your account. If it isn't there, add it as a declared licence. If you are requesting or renewing it on the platform for the championship's year, you can pick the open request: the enrollment waits for the licence. Each driver can be enrolled once per championship. If you renewed elsewhere, declare the new licence first.
3. **Name the Competitor**, if the driver is underage: usually the parent's competitor licence, from the same account.
4. **Pick your race number** (1–999), if the championship offers it. The picker shows which numbers are free, reserved or already used. Your number is held for you until the organizer confirms it.
5. **Choose what you are buying and upload the payment proof**, if enrollment is paid: the enrollment on its own, or a promotional package that includes it, e.g. enrollment and two race fees.
6. **Submit.**

## After submitting

- The organizer confirms enrollments in the order they arrive. Race numbers are first confirmed, first served.
- Until confirmation you can change your race number pick or withdraw the enrollment, from your enrollment on the championship's enrollment page. After it, ask the organizer.
- For a refund of a paid enrollment that is withdrawn or rejected, email the organizer.
- Your category isn't fixed by the enrollment. You choose it at each race registration, and points don't transfer between categories.
