# Configure a new championship

Use this guide when creating a championship that uses enrollment, a specific licence provider or promo codes. Championships that don't need them can skip those steps and work as before.

## Before you start

- Decide whether drivers enroll for a race number, for points, or both.
- Decide what drivers enroll with: free, or a fixed amount, and whether that covers the licence renewal, reserves a race number or gives race fee discounts.

## Steps

1. **Create the championship** with its title and dates.
2. **Choose the licence provider** (ACI or ASI) when creating it, or later from **Accepted licences**, and, if needed, restrict the accepted driver and competitor licences. The provider only names who issues local licences on the forms; drivers can enroll licences from any provider. Without a choice the championship uses the platform-wide provider's name, as before.
3. **Choose the registration form.** Use *complete* for kart, which asks for vehicle and mechanic. Use *standard* or *minimal* for moto.
4. **Set up enrollment** from **Enrollment** on the championship page, if drivers should enroll:
   - turn enrollment on and set when it opens and closes, in the races' timezone;
   - keep *competitor required when underage* on unless the rules say otherwise;
   - choose **Add an option** for each way to enroll: its title, amount (0 for free), whether it covers the licence renewal or reserves a race number, its race fee discount, and the licence categories it is offered to. A championship needs at least one active option.
5. **Set up wildcards**, if only some participants score points: turn wildcards on and pick *based on enrollment*.
6. **Choose how race fees are discounted:**
   - for promo codes and the race fee discounts of enrollment options, turn Entitlements on, which turns bonuses off;
   - otherwise leave bonuses as they are.
7. **Add categories, tyres and races** as usual.

## Check

- Open the public championship page, linked from the enrollment settings. It shows enrollment as open in the configured period, and the options with their amounts. While in early access, only users with early access can open it.
- Start an enrollment with a test account and check that the race number picker appears, if you turned it on.

See [Championship settings](../../reference/championship-settings.md) for every setting and its default.
