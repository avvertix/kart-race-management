# Enroll in a championship and reserve a race number

Enrollment is optional: you can always register for single races. Depending on the championship, enrolling lets you reserve your race number, receive promotional discounts, or score championship points.

1. **Sign in** and open the championship, e.g. from "Open for enrollment" on the dashboard. The form asks one thing at a time, and skips what doesn't apply.
2. **Choose the licence** to enroll, from the ones in your account that aren't enrolled yet. If it isn't there, add it as a declared licence. If you are requesting or renewing it on the platform for the championship's year, you can pick the open request: the enrollment waits for the licence. Without one, choose **Renew for the year**: the enrollment is submitted together with a new licence request, which you complete next. A package including the renewal, or a fee covering it, pays the request too. Each driver can be enrolled once per championship. If you renewed elsewhere, declare the new licence first.
3. **Name the Competitor**, if the driver is underage: usually the parent's competitor licence, from the same account.
4. **Choose what you are buying**, if there is a choice or a fee: the enrollment on its own, or a promotional package that includes it, e.g. enrollment and two race fees.
5. **Pick your race number** (1–999), if the enrollment or the package reserves one. The picker shows which numbers are free, reserved or already used. A licence that already holds a number keeps it. Your number is held for you until the organizer confirms it.
6. **Review and submit.** You land on the enrollment's page. If it is paid, it shows how to pay and where to upload the payment proof.

## After submitting

- The organizer confirms enrollments in the order they arrive. Race numbers are first confirmed, first served.
- Each enrollment has its own page, linked from the championship's enrollment page: licence, race number, payment and status. Until confirmation you can change your race number pick or withdraw the enrollment from there. After it, ask the organizer.
- If the licence request is rejected, the enrollment stays: choose another licence or renew again from its page. What you paid, and the race number, stay with it.
- For a refund of a paid enrollment that is withdrawn or rejected, email the organizer.
- Your category isn't fixed by the enrollment. You choose it at each race registration, and points don't transfer between categories.
