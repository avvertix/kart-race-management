# Notifications

Only the notifications added for licence requests and their orders are listed here. Race registration notifications don't change. Notifications to the applicant go to their account email, which is verified at sign-up. Notifications to the organizer go to the organizer email configured for the platform.

| When | Who is notified |
|---|---|
| A Licence Request is submitted | The applicant (request received) and the organizer |
| An applicant submits again after a change request | The organizer |
| The organizer asks for new documents or changes | The applicant |
| The organizer sets the final price of a range-priced order | The applicant, with the amount, payment details and a link to upload the proof |
| The Licence is emitted | The applicant |

Enrollments, promo codes and orders other than range-priced ones send no notifications for now.
