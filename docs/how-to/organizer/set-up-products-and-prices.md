# Set up products and prices

Licence renewals are not products: drivers start them from **My licences**, and you set the amount of each request while reviewing it. The Licence Types and the documents a request needs are the same for every organizer and are maintained in the application code, see [Add licence providers and platform configuration](../maintainer/add-licence-providers-and-configuration.md).

## Products and Prices

A **Product** is anything sold: a championship enrollment or a **Promotional Package**. Active Products are listed publicly, with their current Price. Each Product has its own public page showing what is included and the price. Share its link in your announcements. Ordering requires a signed-in User with a verified email, and the page explains why.

1. Create the Product and choose its kind. Link it to a championship (enrollment products, optional for packages).
2. Add a **Price** with the dates it applies to and either:
   - a **fixed amount**, charged as is; or
   - a **range** (from–to), when the final amount depends on the order. You confirm the final amount for each order after reviewing it.
   For an early-bird price, add a second Price with earlier dates.

## Promotional Packages

A Package is a Product that grants several things once its Order is paid. For example:

- a licence renewal;
- an enrollment in a championship, with the right to reserve a race number;
- an Entitlement of 2 race fees in that championship.

Add each grant to the Package. Everything is granted when you confirm the payment; what needs a Licence (the Entitlement, the enrollment) is granted once the Licence is emitted.

When a Package includes a licence renewal, the buyer starts the renewal from the paid order: the request shows it is covered by the order and asks for no payment.
