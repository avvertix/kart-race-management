# Request or renew a licence

You need an account. One account can hold requests for several people, e.g. you and your child, or the drivers of your team. Each licence is a separate request.

1. **Open the licences page** and read what each licence needs.
2. **Sign in or create an account.**
3. **Start a request** and choose the licence you need, e.g. driver or mechanic.
4. **Fill in the holder's details.** If the holder is underage, also fill in both parents' details.
5. **Upload the documents** listed for that licence, e.g. identity card, fiscal code and medical certificate.
6. **Download the pre-filled forms** (delegation, privacy, parental authorisation), sign them and upload them.
7. **Pay and upload the payment proof.** If you bought a Promotional Package, its price applies.
8. **Review and submit.**

You can stop at any step: the request stays saved as a draft for as long as you need.

## After submitting

- Follow the request status in your account.
- If the organizer asks for changes, you are notified. Fix only the flagged documents or sections, then submit again.
- When the licence is emitted, download the temporary licence PDF from your account. The licence is now available for enrollments and race registrations.

## Already have a licence?

If you got your licence elsewhere, e.g. renewed directly with the provider or abroad, add it to your account as a declared licence. You can use it right away to enroll; the organizer may verify it.
