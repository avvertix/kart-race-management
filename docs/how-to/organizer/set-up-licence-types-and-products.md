# Set up licence types, products and prices

## Licence Types

Each Licence Provider has its own list of Licence Types, e.g. the ACI driver, competitor and mechanic licences and their sub-types.

1. Pick the provider (ACI or ASI).
2. Add a Licence Type with its name and role (driver, competitor or mechanic).
3. Choose which race registration licence category it counts as, e.g. *national*. This keeps race registration and exports working as they do today.
4. List the documents a request needs. Add the extra documents needed when the holder is underage. For example, for an ACI driver licence:
   - **adult**: identity card and fiscal code, a karting competitive medical certificate, delegation form, privacy form;
   - **underage**: identity card and fiscal code of the minor and of both parents, a karting competitive medical certificate, parental authorisation, delegation form, privacy form signed by both parents.
5. Attach the forms to be signed (delegation, privacy, parental authorisation). They are given to the applicant pre-filled with their details.

## Products and Prices

A **Product** is anything sold: a licence emission or renewal, a championship enrollment, or a **Promotional Package**.

1. Create the Product and choose its kind. Link it to a Licence Type (licence products) or a championship (enrollment products).
2. Add a **Price** with its amount and the dates it applies to. For an early-bird price, add a second Price with earlier dates.
3. For different prices by age or licence category (e.g. junior and senior), create separate Products, one per Licence Type.

## Promotional Packages

A Package is a Product that grants several things once its Order is paid. For example:

- a driver licence renewal;
- an Entitlement of 2 race fees in a championship;
- the right to reserve a race number in that championship.

Add each grant to the Package. Everything is granted when you confirm the payment.
